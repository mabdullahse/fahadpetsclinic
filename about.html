Doctor of Veterinary Medicine

Dr. Sarah Mitchell, DVM

Dr. Sarah Mitchell is a board-certified Doctor of Veterinary Medicine with a passion for preventive care and a gift for connecting with both animals and their owners. Born and raised in Portland, Oregon, she grew up surrounded by animals and knew from childhood that veterinary medicine was her calling.

After graduating summa cum laude from Cornell University College of Veterinary Medicine, Dr. Mitchell completed a prestigious one-year clinical internship in small animal medicine and surgery at the University of Pennsylvania's Ryan Veterinary Hospital. She then joined a multi-specialty practice in Seattle, where she honed her surgical skills and developed a deep interest in integrative veterinary approaches.

In 2016, Dr. Mitchell returned to Portland and founded Fahad Pets Clinic with a single vision: to create a veterinary practice where every pet is treated as family, and where evidence-based medicine is always delivered with empathy and respect.

Our Philosophy

Veterinary Philosophy

Dr. Mitchell's approach to animal care is grounded in science, guided by compassion, and centered on partnership with pet families.

Preventive-First Approach

Prevention is always better — and more affordable — than treatment. Dr. Mitchell emphasizes annual wellness screenings, vaccination, parasite prevention, and lifestyle modifications to keep pets healthy for life.

Compassionate Treatment

Every animal deserves to be treated with kindness, patience, and dignity. Dr. Mitchell uses Fear Free® certified techniques to minimize stress and anxiety for every patient visit, no matter how routine.

Evidence-Based Medicine

Clinical decisions are grounded in the latest peer-reviewed veterinary research. Dr. Mitchell stays current with continuing education and applies proven, scientifically validated treatments to every case.

Client Partnership

Pet owners are essential members of the healthcare team. Dr. Mitchell invests time in educating clients, explaining diagnoses in plain language, and empowering families to make informed decisions about their pet's care.

Holistic Wellness

True pet wellness goes beyond physical health. Dr. Mitchell addresses mental enrichment, behavioral health, nutrition, environmental enrichment, and the human-animal bond as essential components of care.

Integrative Care Options

Alongside conventional medicine, Dr. Mitchell offers integrative modalities including veterinary acupuncture, rehabilitation therapy, and herbal supplementation when scientifically appropriate.

Our Mission

Our Mission at Fahad Pets Clinic

"To provide every pet with the highest standard of compassionate, evidence-based veterinary care — treating each animal as if it were our own, and each client as a trusted partner in their pet's health journey."
