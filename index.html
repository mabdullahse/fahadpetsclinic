Meet Your Veterinarian

Dr. Fahad Aziz

Dr. Fahad is a highly qualified and compassionate Doctor of Veterinary Medicine with over 10 years of experience caring for animals of all shapes and sizes. He earned his DVM and has dedicated his career to providing evidence-based, personalized care for every animal he treats.

His practice philosophy centers on preventive care, early diagnosis, and building lasting relationships. Dr. Fahad believes that educated pet owners make the best healthcare partners.

Why Fahad Pets

Why Choose Our Veterinary Care?

We combine clinical excellence with genuine compassion — because your pets deserve nothing less than the best.

Experienced Veterinary Doctor

Dr. Mitchell brings over a decade of clinical expertise, board certifications, and continuous education to every patient encounter.

Compassionate Pet Care

We treat every pet with the same love and care we'd want for our own — creating a calm, fear-free environment for your animals.

Modern Medical Equipment
