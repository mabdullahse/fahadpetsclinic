Everything Your Pet Needs

From preventive wellness to complex surgical procedures, Fahad Pets Clinic offers the full spectrum of veterinary medicine.

General Veterinary Consultation

A thorough, compassionate physical examination is the cornerstone of our practice. During your pet's consultation, Dr. Mitchell will assess every body system — eyes, ears, mouth, heart, lungs, abdomen, skin, and musculoskeletal structure — while discussing your pet's history, diet, and behavior.

Key Benefits
Early detection of health problems
Personalized health recommendations
Vaccination & parasite prevention review
Nutritional & behavioral guidance

When needed: Annual wellness visits, new pet adoption, any time you notice changes in your pet's health or behavior.

Book Consultation

Vaccination & Immunization

Vaccines are one of the most effective tools for protecting your pet from serious, often fatal diseases. Dr. Mitchell creates personalized vaccination schedules based on your pet's species, age, health status, lifestyle, and geographic location — ensuring optimal protection without unnecessary over-vaccination.

Key Benefits
Protection from rabies, distemper, parvovirus
Customized schedules for each pet
Puppy & kitten series included
Booster reminders and digital records

When needed: Puppies and kittens (series starting at 6–8 weeks), then annually or as recommended by Dr. Mitchell.

Schedule Vaccination

Veterinary Surgery

Our surgical suite is equipped with modern anesthesia monitoring systems, heated surgical tables, and advanced instruments. Dr. Mitchell performs a wide range of soft tissue procedures with precision and careful attention to pain management and post-operative recovery protocols.

Procedures Offered
Spay / neuter procedures
Mass / tumor removal (soft tissue)
GI foreign body removal
Wound repair & reconstructive surgery
Bladder stone removal

When needed: Spay/neuter at appropriate age, when masses are detected, GI obstruction symptoms, or when non-surgical management fails.

Book Surgical Consultation

Veterinary Dental Care

Dental disease is the most common health condition in pets, affecting over 80% of dogs and 70% of cats by age 3. Our dental services include professional cleanings under anesthesia, digital dental X-rays, extractions, and comprehensive oral health assessments to prevent systemic disease caused by oral bacteria.

Key Benefits
Professional scaling & polishing
Full-mouth digital dental X-rays
Safe extractions when necessary
Home dental care guidance

When needed: Annually for most pets, or when you notice bad breath, drooling, reluctance to eat, or visible tartar.

Book Dental Exam

24/7 Emergency Care

Pet emergencies are terrifying — and they happen without warning. Our emergency line is monitored 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Whether it's a late-night toxin ingestion, a traumatic injury, difficulty breathing, or a sudden neurological event, Fahad Pets Clinic is your first call.

Emergency Services
24/7 emergency telephone triage
Trauma & injury management
Toxin ingestion protocols
Respiratory & cardiac emergencies
After-hours specialist referrals

Emergency line: +923076559678 — available 24 hours, 365 days a year.

Call Emergency Line

Advanced Diagnostic Testing

Accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective treatment. Our in-house laboratory delivers bloodwork results within 30 minutes. Our digital radiography and ultrasound imaging systems allow Dr. Mitchell to visualize internal organs and structures with outstanding clarity, often revealing diagnoses invisible to physical examination alone.

Diagnostic Capabilities
Complete blood count & chemistry panel
Urinalysis & fecal screening
Digital full-body radiography
Abdominal & cardiac ultrasound
Cytology & fine needle aspirates

When needed: Pre-surgical screening, illness workup, senior wellness panels, monitoring chronic conditions.

Book Diagnostic Appointment

Pet Nutrition Counseling

What your pet eats directly determines their long-term health, energy, and disease resistance. Dr. Mitchell provides science-based nutritional consultations, evaluating commercial foods, raw diets, and prescription therapeutic diets for pets with specific medical conditions such as kidney disease, diabetes, obesity, or food allergies.

Nutritional Services
Life-stage diet recommendations
Weight management programs
Prescription therapeutic diets
Raw / home-cooked diet evaluation
Food allergy identification

When needed: Overweight pets, pets with medical conditions, puppies/kittens, senior pets, or any time you want to optimize your pet's diet.

Book Nutrition Consult

Parasite Prevention & Control

Fleas, ticks, heartworm, and intestinal parasites pose serious health risks to your pets — and some can be transmitted to humans. Dr. Mitchell will assess your pet's parasite exposure risk and recommend the most effective, safe prevention protocol year-round.

Prevention Programs
Monthly heartworm prevention
Flea & tick prevention strategies
Annual heartworm testing
Intestinal deworming protocols
Tick-borne disease screening

When needed: Year-round prevention is recommended for all dogs and cats, regardless of lifestyle or indoor/outdoor status.

Start a Prevention Plan

Grooming & Wellness Checks

Regular grooming isn't just cosmetic — it's a window into your pet's health. Our wellness grooming appointments include coat and skin inspection, nail trimming, ear cleaning, and anal gland expression, all performed with a clinical eye for abnormalities that owners might miss at home.

Wellness Services
Coat & skin condition assessment
Nail trimming & paw care
Ear cleaning & inspection
Anal gland expression
Body condition scoring

When needed: Every 4–8 weeks depending on breed and coat type, or as part of regular wellness visits.

Book Wellness Visit

Senior Pet Healthcare

Our senior pets deserve special attention. Dogs and cats 7 years and older benefit from biannual checkups and targeted diagnostic panels to detect age-related conditions — arthritis, kidney disease, thyroid disorders, diabetes, cognitive dysfunction, and cancer — at their most treatable stages.

Senior Care Includes
Bi-annual comprehensive exams
Geriatric bloodwork panels
Arthritis & mobility assessment
Cognitive function screening
End-of-life comfort planning

When needed: Dogs and cats 7 years and older should visit every 6 months for comprehensive senior wellness screening.

Book Senior Wellness Exam
