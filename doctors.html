Veterinary Professionals

Our Expert Veterinary Team

Every member of our team shares a deep commitment to animal welfare and evidence-based veterinary medicine.

Small Animal Medicine & Surgery

Dr. Fahad Aziz

DVM - Founder & Chief Veterinarian

Dr. Fahad founded Fahad Pets Clinic in 2016 after completing his DVM at Cornell University and a clinical internship at UPenn. He specializes in soft tissue surgery, diagnostic imaging, and integrative veterinary medicine. His Fear Free® certification ensures every patient visit is as stress-free as possible.

Internal Medicine & Emergency

Dr. Faiz ul Hassan

DVM - Master in Theriogenology

Aspiring Veterinary Embryologist with large-scale dairy farm management experience and expertise in livestock management, herd health, milking, TMR/feed formulation, semen cryopreservation, IVF, ICSI, OPU, and embryo transfer.

Support Staff

Our Dedicated Support Team

Behind every great veterinarian is an incredible team of registered vet nurses, technicians, and client care coordinators.

Registered Veterinary Nurses

Our team of 4 registered veterinary nurses assists with procedures, administers medications, performs pre-surgical preparation, and provides compassionate recovery care.

Veterinary Technicians

Skilled in venipuncture, digital radiography, laboratory processing, anesthesia monitoring, and dental prophylaxis. All hold AVT certification.

Client Care Coordinators

Our front desk and care coordination team ensures seamless scheduling, insurance assistance, follow-up communication, and a warm welcome for every pet and family.
